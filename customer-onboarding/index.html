Architecture Question

How do we turn fragmented lending activity into one coherent onboarding capability?

The problem was larger than the customer interface. Successful onboarding required alignment across product guidance, banker enablement, eligibility, customer information, KYC/FICA, document readiness, credit and risk, case management, fulfilment, legacy validation and servicing readiness.

The architecture therefore needed to answer not only what the customer or banker sees, but which capabilities perform the work, what information is required, which systems and services support each step, who owns exceptions, and how the customer becomes ready for ongoing servicing.

Capability Architecture

Onboarding is a chain of reusable business capabilities.

Product Discovery & Guidance

Help the customer and banker understand the product, suitability, requirements and next action.

Eligibility

Determine whether customer and product conditions support progression before unnecessary fulfilment effort begins.

Customer & Compliance

Document Readiness

Determine what evidence is required, what has been received, what is valid and what remains outstanding.

Evaluate risk, approval conditions, exceptions and decision outcomes.

Fulfilment

Information Architecture

Model the business objects before mapping them to systems.

The architecture separates the meaning of an information object from the technology currently used to store or process it. This becomes important when defining ownership, reusable services and integration responsibilities.

Customer

Identity, profile, relationship and customer reference information.

Product

Lending proposition, rules, requirements and product conditions.

Application

Customer request, selected product, current onboarding state and application progression.

Eligibility Result

Structured outcome determining whether the customer proceeds, is referred or stops.

Compliance Result

Verification, regulatory and policy outcome used by the onboarding process.

Document

Required evidence, receipt status, validation state and document readiness.

Credit Decision

Approval, decline, conditions, risk assessment and exception reason.

Case

Operational work container containing ownership, tasks, status, exceptions and next action.

Architecture Decisions

The solution is shaped through explicit trade-offs.

Treat onboarding as a business capability rather than a channel-specific journey

The same underlying capabilities should support banker-led, assisted and future digital experiences.

Separate customer experience concerns from operational workflow concerns

A simple front-stage experience should not hide unclear ownership, duplicated work or weak operational controls.

Reuse existing enterprise capabilities before introducing new ones

Existing services, case-management patterns and platform capabilities should be assessed before creating duplicate functionality.

Establish servicing readiness during onboarding

Onboarding is not complete simply because fulfilment succeeded. Customer and product information must support the ongoing servicing relationship.

Treat compliance as integrated journey logic

Compliance requirements should shape information capture, validation and progression rather than appearing as a late-stage blocker.

Separate integration orchestration from system ownership

The iPaaS extension explores how systems remain responsible for their business data while orchestration coordinates activity between them.

System Ownership

Source systems remain responsible for their business information. Middleware does not become a new system of record.

Reusable Service Boundaries

Eligibility, compliance, document, credit and fulfilment capabilities are treated as reusable services, not journey-specific integrations.

Transformation & Routing

Information can be mapped, transformed and routed between systems without exposing technical complexity to the banker.

Exception Architecture

Timeouts, negative decisions, missing information and technical failures become explicit operational states requiring ownership and recovery.

Proposed POC

A first pilot could validate: onboarding request → Flowgear → eligibility service → decision → operational case update.
