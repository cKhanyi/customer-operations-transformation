An end-to-end banking architecture case study connecting customer intent, business capabilities, operational processes, information objects, enterprise services, systems and controls.

I subsequently extended the architecture through an iPaaS lens using Flowgear to explore how eligibility, KYC/FICA, documents, credit, fulfilment, core systems and servicing could be orchestrated through reusable integration boundaries rather than point-to-point dependencies.
