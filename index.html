How I Work

Start with the operating problem, not the interface.

I approach transformation by understanding the capability the organisation needs, the people involved, the process being executed, the information moving through it, the services supporting it, and the systems responsible for delivery.

This helps distinguish a customer-experience problem from a process problem, a process problem from an information problem, and an information problem from a technology gap.
